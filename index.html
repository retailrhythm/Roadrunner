Development & Regulatory Context

Legacy Status & Existing Use

The seller has had preliminary discussions with Hays County regarding the property's long-standing use. The tract has existed as an unplatted property since the 1970s and is believed to fall under legacy conditions that predate certain current subdivision and on-site utility regulations.

Properties in this category are often recognized in their existing configuration, even if they would not meet today's standards for subdivision, well spacing, or septic permitting. This can provide flexibility from a "grandfathered use" standpoint.

Key Considerations

  • On-site sewage facilities fall under TCEQ rules, administered locally by the county. Older systems installed prior to current regulations are often allowed to remain in service if functioning.
  • New systems or expansions generally need to comply with current TCEQ standards.
  • Well placement and additional utility improvements are subject to current spacing and permitting requirements.
  • Initial feedback from Hays County was favorable regarding the property's existing use.
  • Any future changes, additional improvements, or reconfiguration should be independently verified by a buyer with Hays County and the appropriate authorities.
